# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Action Translation is an iPhone and iPad app that translates text using an AI provider you choose. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## In short

- The developer of Action Translation does not collect, receive, or store your data. The app has no accounts, servers, ads, analytics, or tracking.
- When you start a translation, your text goes directly from your device to the AI provider you selected.
- Your translation history, settings, and API keys are kept on your device.

## What is sent to your AI provider

By adding an API key in Settings, you allow the app to send the data described below to that provider. To withdraw your permission, remove the key.

Nothing is sent until you start a translation. When you do, the app sends the following directly to the provider selected in Settings (Google, Anthropic, or OpenAI):

- the text you want to translate;
- the translation instructions, including the model, the target language, the source language if you chose one, and the language of the app's interface;
- your API key, which identifies you to the provider.

Like any internet service, the provider also receives technical connection data, such as your IP address.

Your provider handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy and under your agreement with it. Terms can differ by plan: for example, some free plans allow the provider to use submitted content to improve its products. Check your provider's terms before translating confidential or personal information.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)
- [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)

## What stays on your device

- **Translation history:** your last 50 translations, including the source text, the result, the languages, the model, the date, and the response time. Older translations are deleted automatically.
- **Settings:** your provider, models, target language, and the languages you use most.
- **API keys:** stored in the iOS Keychain.

The app and its Share extension use this data together on your device. The app does not sync it to any server, but it may be included in device backups according to your iOS settings.

## Content from other apps and device access

The Share extension receives only the text or link you choose to share with it. The app writes to the clipboard only when you tap Copy. It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

## Deleting your data

- To delete a translation, swipe it in the history.
- To remove an API key, clear its field in Settings.
- Deleting the app removes your history and settings. iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so clear your API keys first.
- To access or delete data held by your AI provider, contact that provider.

## Apple and this website

Apple handles App Store downloads under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give the developer crash and usage reports that do not identify you.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses. The website itself uses no cookies or analytics.

## Children

The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

If you have questions, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/claustrofob/actiontranslation/issues). Issues are public, so do not include personal information or API keys. For privacy requests, email [action.translation.app@gmail.com](mailto:action.translation.app@gmail.com).
