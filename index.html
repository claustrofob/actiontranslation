For iPhone and iPad with iOS 27 or later

Stay in what you’re reading

  1. Select a word, a phrase, or a whole page of text in any app.
  2. Tap Share, then Translate.
  3. Read the result in a sheet. Edit the text, correct the language, or copy the translation.

A word gets a dictionary entry. A sentence gets a translation.

The app works out what you sent. For a word or expression you get the normal form, synonyms, part of speech, numbered meanings, and usage examples. Longer text comes back as a plain, natural translation.

Your last 50 translations stay under the result, so you can open or repeat any of them.

Use the AI provider you already trust

Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or OpenAI

Add your own API key in Settings and pick a model. There is no Action Translation account and no server in between: each request goes from your device to your provider, and any usage charges come from your plan with that provider.

Your translations aren’t ours to see

The app has no ads, analytics, or tracking. History and settings stay on your device, and API keys stay in the iOS Keychain. The text you translate goes only to the provider you chose.
